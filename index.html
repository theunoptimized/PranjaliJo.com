Work

Bringing AI
to market.

At NVIDIA, I lead product marketing for Cosmos, a platform for world foundation models. I work directly with researchers and product teams on how these models reach developers.

That means understanding what a model can do and where it falls short; deciding who it’s useful for; and giving developers a reason to try it.

Product strategy

Which capabilities matter to customers, and what should the product make possible?

World Modeldesk.

World models, made understandable.

World Modeldesk is my independent initiative to explain world models without assuming you already know the field.

I break down the research: how these models represent the world, what the results tell us, and what they might mean for physical AI.
